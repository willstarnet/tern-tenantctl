# Getting help

- **How do I ...?** Check the [README](README.md) (what is claimed, `TENANTCTL_OUTPUT`), then
  open a [question](https://github.com/willstarnet/tern-tenantctl/issues/new?template=question.md).
- **Something is broken or reads wrong.** Open a
  [bug report](https://github.com/willstarnet/tern-tenantctl/issues/new?template=bug_report.md)
  with the plugin, Tern and tenantctl versions, the command and what you expected. Replace tenant
  data (names, addresses, ids, IPs) in anything you paste.
- **An idea.** Open a
  [feature request](https://github.com/willstarnet/tern-tenantctl/issues/new?template=feature_request.md).
- **Security.** Privately, as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) describes.
- **tenantctl itself** (wrong data, sign-in, permissions):
  [tenantctl's issues](https://github.com/willstarnet/tenantctl/issues).
- **Tern itself** (crashes, the plugin API): see the [Tern docs](https://docs.stencil.so/tern/).
